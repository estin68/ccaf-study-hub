🧠 Study Daily Loop (Cornell + Feynman)

Use the Cornell note template then the Feynman loop below. They reinforce each other.

CORNELL

Left = Cues · Right = Notes · Bottom = Summary

While reading the guide, jot key terms/questions on the left; your answers/details on the right; then a 2–3 sentence summary. Turns passive reading into active recall.

FEYNMAN

1 Teach · 2 Find gaps · 3 Recap · 4 Simplify

Explain the topic in plain words (out loud or to someone). Wherever you stumble, that's a gap → go back to the guide. Repeat until you can teach it simply.

✅ Pass Strategy & Daily Tips

  • Day 1–2 build the mental model (why Claude behaves the way it does).
  • Day 3–6 one strong topic per day + its quiz drill — don't binge questions without notes.
  • Active recall > re-reading: cover the notes column and answer the cues; only peek to verify.
  • Spaced repetition: each morning quickly re-cue the previous days' summaries before new content.
  • Day 7: run the full practice exam timed, then re-read every explanation — even for correct answers.
  • Aim 80% and above twice in a row before exam day for confidence.
